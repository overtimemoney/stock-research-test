Projection Builder

Year-by-year EPS — the first row is the TTM (the last twelve months, already reported, with its actual growth); the rest is the analysts' forecast path, auto-filled when you load a stock. Every box is editable: change an EPS and its growth % updates; change a growth % and that year's EPS updates. * = extrapolated past the last analyst year, with growth fading toward the long-run rate instead of repeating the last year forever.

Bear = each year's growth −5 pts & exit P/E −2 · Base = the path above · Bull = growth +5 pts & exit P/E +2. Projections are only as good as the assumptions — they're yours, not a forecast.
